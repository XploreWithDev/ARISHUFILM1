Four Days Left

Drama · Short Film · 2025 | Written & Directed by Hariprasath Venkatesan

Four Days Left is a film born from real emotion — the kind that builds slowly, quietly, under the surface of everyday life.

It's about the feeling of being stuck between two worlds. The pressure of timelines, rules, and choices that no one prepares you for. The loneliness that comes with keeping it all together while everything feels like it's falling apart.

"This isn't a film filled with spectacle. It's filled with silence, hesitation, unspoken love, and the weight of decisions made in small rooms."

I made this film for those navigating uncertain paths. For those who've had to make life-changing decisions with no clear right answer. And for those who understand that sometimes, strength is quiet.

If You Say Yes

Screenlife Romantic Drama · 2025 | Written & Directed by Hariprasath Venkatesan

If You Say Yes is a screenlife romantic short film that unfolds entirely through video calls, chats, and glowing laptop screens. When Sam finally confesses his love to his best friend Max, what starts as a simple text spirals into a tense, funny, and deeply intimate night of waiting.

As Sam anxiously replays every moment and Max wrestles with her feelings, their friends become silent witnesses to a modern love story shaped by distance, memory, and vulnerability.

"Told in real time through digital windows, the film explores the fragile space between friendship and love — the fear of rejection, the comfort of familiarity, and the courage it takes to say yes."

Quiet, heartfelt, and relatable, If You Say Yes captures how today's relationships live and breathe through screens, yet still carry the same timeless emotions.

Just a Ride: Different Roads, Same Heart (Development)

Writer: Arishu Films • July 26, 2025 • 1 min read

by Hariprasath Venkatesan

"Some journeys don't need distance — just a change in perspective."

Just a Ride is a quiet reflection on connection, discomfort, and the quiet power of conversation. It's about what happens when two people from different worlds share a few moments of honesty on a silent night.

No drama. No preaching. Just a reminder: we're all trying to find our way. And sometimes, the road teaches us more than the destination ever could.
